## Part 2 — Financing options
- **IMM Invest / IMM Plus (FNGCIMM)** — 2025-26 = **IMM Plus**, budget **RON 12.5B**, to **13 Dec 2026**. Guarantee ≤90%; max RON 5M working capital / RON 10M investment; ~ROBOR 3M +1.5–1.9%. Six sub-schemes: IMM România Plus, Agro Plus, IMM Prod Plus, Construct Plus, Rural Plus, Innovation Plus. New refinancing facility (max RON 4M, ≤80%). Key for bridging grant beneficiaries.
- **EIF/EIB (InvestEU RO compartment, PNRR)** — ~€672M envelope; €610M+ already reached 2,000+ RO SMEs via 12 banks → lower rates/less collateral/longer tenor.
- **Bank credit** — revolving working-capital + term investment (≤72 mo), IRCC/ROBOR + margin; often + IMM Plus guarantee.
- **Leasing** — financial vs operational; ~€2.46B operational / ~€3B financial, ~60% auto.
- **Factoring** — invoice finance; **€10.5B in 2025 (+12%)**, 88% domestic; ~2–3% + interest.
- **Microfinance / IFN** — Patria Credit IFN, BT Mic (EIF-backed), Agricover — under-banked micro/rural.
- **VC & angels** — GapMinder Fund II (€80M), Early Game II (€60M), Catalyst II (€50M), Sparking Capital II (~€30M); TechAngels; many co-financed by EIF PNRR Recovery Equity FoF.
- **Equity crowdfunding** — **SeedBlink** (ECSPR/ASF-authorized, EU-passported), €80M+ AUM, 200+ deals. ECSPR cap €5M/issuer/12mo.

## Part 3 — End-to-end consultancy workflow (→ product stages)
Everything for the €46B 2021-27 Cohesion funds runs through **MySMIS2021** (AFIR portal for rural/FEADR). 2021-27 change: submit a **declarație unică** first; upload full docs at contracting if admitted.
1. **Eligibility screening** — applicant (legal form, size via IMM + *întreprindere unică*, age, positive equity, no state debts, not insolvent, eligible CAEN) + project (eligible activities/area/value, expense categories, *effect stimulativ*, aid-scheme fit).
2. **Program fit** — map idea → open/upcoming *apel*; pre-check scoring grid.
3. **Application dossier** — Cererea de finanțare (MySMIS master form); plan de afaceri / studiu de fezabilitate; financial statements; declarații (eligibilitate, IMM, întreprindere unică, dublă finanțare, ajutor de stat, cofinanțare); buget/deviz + quotes; ownership docs; administrative (certificat constatator ONRC, act constitutiv, fiscal certs, certificat de urbanism).
4. **Business plan** — description, market analysis, SMART objectives + Gantt, buget indicativ (eligible vs ineligible), **multi-year projections over durability horizon (impl. + 3–5 yr)**, indicatori de rezultat/realizare (become contractual obligations).
5. **Co-financing / costs / VAT** — grant ≤~90%; eligible vs ineligible per guide; **VAT eligible only if genuinely, definitively borne & non-recoverable** (VAT-payer's VAT ineligible even if not reclaimed); reimbursement-based → need bridge credit / scrisoare de garanție bancară (FNGCIMM ≤~80%).
6. **Evaluation** — conformity/eligibility (+ clarificări) → technical-financial scoring on grila de evaluare (~4 weighted criteria) → threshold + ranking until budget exhausted.
7. **Contracting** — upload docs; sign contract de finanțare in MySMIS; fixes budget/indicators/disbursement/reporting/durability; post bank guarantee for advance.
8. **Implementation & reporting** — prefinanțare/avans or rambursare; cerere de plată in MySMIS with invoices/proofs. Procurement: private → Ordin MFE 1284/2016 (≥2 bidders); public → Legea 98/2016; external → PRAG. Periodic reports + on-site visits.
9. **Audit & durability** — min **3 yr SMEs / 5 yr large & public** from final payment; keep assets, maintain indicators, retain docs; annual rapoarte de durabilitate + ex-post visits; audits by AM/Audit Authority/EC/ECA/DLAF-OLAF. Breach → financial corrections up to full recovery.

Consultancy fee model: fixed writing fee + **success fee (% of awarded grant)**.

## Part 4 — Matching-engine data model (7 filters, all computable from ERP data)
| Filter | Source | Logic |
|---|---|---|
| **CAEN** (Rev.3 + Rev.2 map) | ONRC main + secondary | On call whitelist AND not blacklist. Rev.3 from 1 Jan 2025; transition to 25 Sep 2026 → run both Rev.3 + Rev.2 antecedents. Common exclusions: primary agriculture (01), fishing (03), coal (05), tobacco (12), arms (2540/3040), gambling (92), real estate (68), financial (64–66). |
| **NUTS region** | Company/site county → NUTS-2 | Drives co-fin %: 7/8 regions "less developed" → up to 85% EU; **București-Ilfov (RO32) → ~40%**. Codes RO11–RO42. |
| **Enterprise size** (Rec. 2003/361, Legea 346/2004) | Headcount + turnover + balance, **consolidated over partner (25–50% pro-rata)/linked (>50% 100%)** | Micro (<10, ≤€2M) / Small (<50, ≤€10M) / Medium (<250, ≤€50M turnover or ≤€43M balance). SME aid bonus (+10pp medium, +20pp small). **Never evaluate applicant in isolation.** |
| **Project type** | Intended activity/expenditure | Digitalization/green/equipment/R&D/training/internationalization → call intervention field + GBER article. |
| **Age / turnover / profitability** | ONRC reg date + ANAF bilanț | Usually ≥1 (sometimes ≥3) fiscal years; positive operating profit; positive equity; **not "firm in difficulty"** (GBER Art.2(18)); no ANAF debts; not insolvent; no Deggendorf order. |
| **De minimis headroom** | RegAS + self-declaration over single undertaking | **€300,000 − rolling 3-yr total ≥ requested?** From 1 Jan 2026 all de minimis in central register; from 31 Mar 2027 exclusively via RegAS (automated). |
| **Legal basis** | Call metadata | de minimis (→ €300k check) vs **GBER 651/2014** article (→ ceiling + regional/SME intensity + sectoral exclusions) vs notified scheme. Max grant = eligible costs × intensity, capped by ceiling/headroom. |

## Part 5 — Feature decomposition (→ sprint stories)
1. **Program catalog/registry** (national + EU records: purpose, beneficiary, size, co-fin %, legal basis, deadlines, CAEN whitelist/blacklist, regions, intervention field) — needs a maintenance process (calls open/close continuously).
2. **Matching engine** — 7 filters vs ERP data → ranked eligible calls + estimated max grant + co-fin.
3. **De minimis ledger** — cumulative per single undertaking, rolling 3-yr; reconcile with RegAS; headroom alerts.
4. **Eligibility pre-check wizard** — free screening front door; pass/fail per criterion with reasons (leverages accounting data for "firm in difficulty"/equity/ANAF-debt).
5. **Dossier checklist generator** — per-call document list + upload tracking.
6. **Business-plan + financial-projection builder** — aligned to scoring grid; multi-year P&L/cash-flow over durability horizon; indicator targets. *(reuses the Business-Case engine.)*
7. **Co-financing / VAT-eligibility calculator** — beneficiary contribution, eligible vs ineligible, VAT eligibility.
8. **Financing marketplace** — IMM Plus sub-schemes, bridge credit / scrisoare de garanție, leasing, factoring, VC/crowdfunding matched to the grant's cash-flow profile.
9. **Implementation & durability tracker** — cereri de rambursare, Ordin 1284/2016 procurement files, reporting cadence, 3–5 yr durability calendar + alerts (ties to existing alerts/calendar).
10. **MySMIS2021 / AFIR integration** — submission + status tracking (aspirational; confirm API).

## Verification flags
- **PNRR digitalization CLOSED** — do not present as an open opportunity.
- **Femeia Antreprenor & Microindustrializare** budget-constrained 2025 (risk = payment timing).
- **Start-Up Nation ceiling** (200k vs 250k lei) edition-dependent.
- **Regional program sizes/co-fin %** set per-call by each ADR — verify each Ghidul Solicitantului at launch.
- **EIC equity** standard cap €10M; ~€15M only via STEP Scale-Up.
- **CAEN Rev.2↔Rev.3 table mandatory** to build (transition through Sep 2026).
- All figures point-in-time — catalog needs live updates.

04

Self-evolving intelligence layer

A governed multi-agent system that improves the platform — researching regulation, drafting modules, hardening tests, watching ops. It is deliberately firewalled from the statutory execution path.

◆ FIREWALL

Hard architectural boundary. Self-evolving agents may propose, research, draft code, and open PRs — they may never post a tax filing, alter a VAT rate, or approve a close. Every output touching compliance passes through deterministic validators and the human romanian-tax-specialist veto. Autonomy is scoped to the platform's own codebase, research, and monitoring — never a customer's ledger. This directly answers the AI-liability risk (§09).

CORAL

Custom Δ

Multi-agent self-evolution orchestrator — the conductor. Spawns, evaluates and retires specialist agents against fitness functions (tests pass, coverage up, latency down).

Role: orchestration brain

AutoResearchClaw

Custom Δ

Autonomous research agent (researchclaw). Watches ANAF / EU tax sources, summarises rule changes into structured diffs for the tax-specialist to ratify.

Role: regulatory radar

EvoAgentX

OSS Δ

Open framework for evolving agent workflows. Candidate engine for CORAL's evolution loop — mutate → evaluate → select on module-generation tasks.

Role: evolution engine

Hermes Agent

OSS Δ

Nous Research tool-using agent. Strong general executor for scoped build/refactor tasks under CORAL supervision.

Role: builder agent

ScienceClaw

OSS Δ

MIT research agent (lamm-mit). Hypothesis-driven — fits forecasting/anomaly model R&D and controlled experiments on the platform's own data.

Role: research scientist

Xagent

Custom Δ

Trading & scouting agent, repurposed as an ops scout — surfaces cost/perf regressions and integration health across services.

Role: ops sentinel

Maturity is honest: these are a mix of private prototypes (CORAL, AutoResearchClaw, Xagent) and early open-source research projects (EvoAgentX, Hermes, ScienceClaw, mercury-agent, Feynman). None is battle-tested in a regulated financial context. The layer therefore ships as a governed R&D track (Horizon E) with staged autonomy gates — read-only → PR-proposing → auto-merge-behind-tests — never skipping straight to production authority. Complementary Claude-Code tooling (OneRedOak review/security workflows, curated skills libraries, an llm-wiki-style session→knowledge promotion) becomes the agents' shared toolbelt and memory.

Grounded in the actual server, not the aspiration. This is what runs where — and the model to start with today.

⚠ REALITY

The current host has no GPU. It is an 8-core Intel i7-7700 (2017), 62 GB RAM, ~500 GB free. Ollama is installed but its model list is almost entirely :cloud (remote) — the only local weight file is a FLUX image model. Every "GPU acceleration" claim in prior docs is aspirational. The plan below works today on this box and scales when a GPU is provisioned.

08

Go-to-market & business model

The tech is necessary, not sufficient. #1 is won through the channel — and in Romanian accounting, the channel is the accountant.

Wedge

Accountant-led distribution

One accounting firm serves 50–200 SMBs. Win the firm with multi-client filing + white-label, and you acquire its whole book at near-zero marginal CAC. This is the flywheel, not paid ads.

Land

Free compliance, paid control

Free tier: e-Factura + SAF-T + OCR (the painful mandatory stuff). Paid: Pro 49 / Business 149 RON/mo for Controlling, HR, consolidation. Compliance is the hook; control is the revenue.

Expand

Three revenue lines

SaaS subscription + consultancy marketplace take-rate + usage (API, bulk filing, GPU jobs). NRR >120% by climbing Pro→Business→advisory within each account.

Defensibility (the real moat): not "we did compliance first" — SAP can localise. It is (1) the accountant network and its switching cost, (2) accumulated ledger + document data that trains better OCR/forecasts than anyone else has for RO SMBs, and (3) the self-evolution layer compounding feature velocity. Each quarter the gap widens; that is what makes #1 durable rather than merely first.

09

Work & delivery platform

Beyond running the books: help members find work, grow, and deliver it — then get paid compliantly, without leaving the platform. ~70% assembles from modules that already exist.

↻ CLOSED LOOP

The loop no RO competitor closes: match → contract auto-drafted → delivery workspace provisioned → client + team collaborate → deliver in-platform → client approves milestone → escrow releases + e-Factura issued → income & tax tracked. Upwork/eJobs stop at "matched"; delivery, IP, review and payment stay native here.

01

Talent & gig marketplace

Post jobs/gigs; profiles carry resume, availability, skills & rates. Matching = hard filters + local embeddings (free, on-box). Reuses ats, freelancer, crm.

02

Growth layer

Career trajectories (skill-gap → next role) → recommend training (the lms already exists, 20 files) → book a coach (reuses the consulting booking engine). Flywheel: match → train → coach → re-match at a higher rate.

03

Delivery workspaces

Per-project dev container / shared VM / GPU pod where client + freelancers/teams connect and build. Provisioned via your proven RunPod flow; compute is pass-through to the client — near-zero cost to the platform.

Monetization (four lines, low marginal cost): gig take-rate / lead fee · coaching take-rate · training subscriptions · workspace compute pass-through + platform fee — and the real prize, converting marketplace members onto paid invoicing/ERP tiers.

Cold-start cheat: you already build a funnel of both sides — freelancers/PFAs arriving for free invoicing, SMBs for the ERP.
